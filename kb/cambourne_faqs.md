## FAQs

### Where can I find prayer times?

Use `knowledge/prayer_times_cambourne.csv` for extracted daily rows, and verify live at https://www.cambournecrescent.org/service/prayer-times/ for operational changes.

### Where is the timetable location?

The prayer page says times are sourced for Cambourne at 52.2178° N, 0.0662° W.

### What is the Jumu'ah khutbah time?

The prayer page displays Jum'ah Khutbah: 1:10 pm. Verify the live page for current Friday arrangements.

### What are the prayer locations?

The prayer page lists Blue Space (BS), Cambourne Village College (CVC), HUB, Lower Cricket Pavilion (LCP), New Cricket Pavilion (NCP), and Sports Pavilion (SP). The timetable rows may use variants such as BS-1 or BS-H.

### How much prayer data is available?

The latest generation extracted 221 dated prayer rows into the CSV.

### How should missing jamaat times be handled?

If a jamaat or location field is blank in the CSV, answer that the source page does not specify it for that prayer/date and point the user to the live prayer page.
